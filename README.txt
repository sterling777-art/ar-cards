AR card deck, made with Deck Press
===================================

What's in here
  index.html     the page people open on their phone
  deck.json      card list and model settings
  targets.mind   what the camera looks for (made from your drawings)
  cards/         drawings used for tracking
  models/        3D models (.glb)
  print/         drawings for printing
  qr.png         QR code for your site (if you entered the address)

Put it online with GitHub Pages (free)
  1. Sign in at github.com and click New repository. Name it (for example ar-cards). Set it to Public.
  2. On the new repository page, click "uploading an existing file".
     Drag in everything from this folder, including the cards, models and print folders. Click Commit changes.
  3. Go to Settings > Pages. Under Source pick "Deploy from a branch", then main and / (root). Save.
  4. After about a minute your link shows at the top of that page, for example https://yourname.github.io/ar-cards/

Use it
  Open the link on a phone in Safari or Chrome, tap Start, allow the camera, and hold up a printed card.
  Print on matte paper at 100% size. Good, even light helps tracking.

Add more cards later
  Open this zip in Deck Press, add the new drawings and models, export again, and upload the new files over the old ones.
